Implement `find_duplicates(items)` in solution.py.

Return a list of the values that appear more than once in `items`, each listed
once, in the order they first become a duplicate (i.e. ordered by the position
of their second occurrence). It must also work when the elements are unhashable
(e.g. lists), not just hashable values.

Examples:
  find_duplicates([1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3]) == [2, 3]
  find_duplicates([[1], [1], [2]])    == [[1]]
